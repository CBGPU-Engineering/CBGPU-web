# Code of conduct

We want students, researchers and developers to be able to participate respectfully,
regardless of experience, background, identity or disability.

- Be considerate, constructive and patient. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information or private school material.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make sexual advances or repeatedly disrupt
  discussions. Do not retaliate against someone who reports a concern.
- Accept reasonable moderation and keep conversations relevant to the project.

This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces. Maintainers
may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict participation or ban users
according to the severity and recurrence of misconduct. Maintainers follow these
rules as well and should explain moderation decisions when doing so preserves
privacy and safety.

Report concerns privately to **lmw.hpc@gmail.com**. Include links and enough context
to investigate; reports will be handled discreetly on a best-effort basis. A public
explanation will not reveal a reporter's identity without permission.
